VoiceMorph Studio Privacy Policy

Enterprise Privacy Framework • 100% Offline Zero-Knowledge Architecture • Vocal Sovereignty by Design

Last Updated: November 14, 2026

Privacy First Commitment: VoiceMorph Studio has been engineered around a strict 100% Offline Zero-Knowledge Architecture. Your live microphone audio, morphed voice streams, dual-side call recordings, WhatsApp voice notes, transformed audio files, and custom vocal preferences remain under your exclusive local control and are never transmitted to Accessible Tech Labs India or any third-party cloud server for storage, analysis, biometric profiling, or monetization.

Accessible Tech Labs India is committed to protecting the privacy, vocal confidentiality, and digital sovereignty of every user. This Privacy Policy explains how VoiceMorph Studio operates, how microphone and audio loopback streams are processed locally on your computer, how your personal audio files remain protected, and the architectural principles that govern the application. Unlike cloud-based AI voice changers that upload your voice biometrics to remote servers, VoiceMorph Studio is built on a foundational principle: your voice and your conversations belong exclusively to you.

VoiceMorph Studio is not a surveillance tool, cloud voice harvester, advertising network, or behavioral analytics tracker. The application is engineered strictly to provide real-time local DSP voice transformation, accessible call recording, and local voice note creation while eliminating external data exposure.

1. Zero-Knowledge Offline Architecture

VoiceMorph Studio operates under a strict Zero-Knowledge Offline Architecture model. Under this architecture, Accessible Tech Labs India does not possess the technical capability, server infrastructure, or network hooks to listen to, monitor, intercept, recover, analyze, or access your microphone input, live calls, recorded conversations, or saved voice notes.

All digital signal processing (DSP) operations—including granular pitch shifting, BiQuad formant filtering, noise gating, studio peak limiting, acoustic echo generation, and MP3/WAV encoding—occur 100% locally in real time on your Windows CPU without routing any audio data over the internet.

Because our architecture is completely serverless, Accessible Tech Labs India maintains zero databases containing your voiceprints, vocal biometrics, call recordings, voice notes, or application usage logs.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

VoiceMorph Studio does not collect, record, upload, log, sell, rent, license, monetize, or share any of the following categories of personal, vocal, or behavioral information:

  • Live microphone audio or vocal biometric signatures
  • Dual-side live call recordings or conversation transcripts
  • Recorded WhatsApp or Telegram voice notes
  • Existing audio files selected for local voice transformation
  • Names, usernames, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • Calling application histories, contact lists, or chat logs
  • Passwords, browser cookies, or account credentials
  • Banking, UPI, or payment card details
  • Personal files, folders, or private documents
  • Geographic location or IP tracking logs
  • Behavioral analytics, crash telemetry, or usage statistics
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking tokens

Accessible Tech Labs India does not embed third-party advertisements inside VoiceMorph Studio and never sells or shares user data with any third party.

3. Microphone Access and Real-Time Audio Routing Privacy

VoiceMorph Studio requires access to your physical microphone strictly to perform real-time voice transformation and local voice note recording when explicitly initiated by you.

User-Controlled Activation: Microphone capture is only active when you explicitly start the Real-Time Live Voice Changer (Alt + L / Alt + Shift + L), initiate a Quick 10-Second Voice Check (Alt + T), start Self-Test Preview in Settings, or record a Voice Note (Alt + R). You can mute your live microphone stream at any time using the global shortcut (Alt + Shift + X) or stop the engine completely.

Automatic Virtual Cable Parking: Whenever Live Mode is turned off or the application exits, VoiceMorph Studio automatically parks the virtual audio cable in a disabled standby state and restores your physical microphone and physical speakers as the Windows default endpoints.

4. Dual-Side Live Call Recording and Voice Note Storage

VoiceMorph Studio provides an optional Dual-Side Live Call Recorder (Alt + C / Alt + Shift + R) and a Voice Note Studio (Alt + R) for user convenience.

When you activate Live Call Recording during an active Live Mode session, the application mixes your outgoing morphed microphone stream with the incoming caller audio from your physical speaker loopback and streams the recording directly to a local file on your hard drive. Temporary working WAV buffers created in your local Windows temporary directory during recording or 10-second voice checks are automatically cleaned up once processing finishes.

Finalized call recordings, voice notes, and transformed audio files are saved exclusively inside your designated local folder (defaulting to your Downloads\VoiceMorph Studio directory). Accessible Tech Labs India never uploads, backs up, or accesses these files.

5. Local Configuration and Startup Preferences

Application preferences—such as your selected visual theme, preferred input/output audio device IDs, active voice profile, custom pitch and formant slider values, studio echo settings, and default save folder path—are stored solely on your computer inside a local JSON configuration file located in your Windows Local Application Data directory (LocalApplicationData\Accessible Tech Labs India\VoiceMorphStudio\settings.json).

If you enable the optional "Run automatically in background when Windows starts" preference, VoiceMorph Studio creates a local startup launcher in your Windows Startup directory so the application can start in standby mode upon login. You can disable this option at any time in Settings, which immediately removes the local startup launcher.

6. Virtual Microphone Driver Setup (Network Usage)

VoiceMorph Studio performs zero network requests during normal operation, live voice changing, call recording, or audio file transformation.

The only time the application initiates an internet connection is when you explicitly authorize the automatic installation of the free Virtual Microphone Driver (VB-Cable). During that one-time setup, the application downloads the official driver package directly from VB-Audio Software's official distribution server (download.vb-audio.com) into a temporary local folder, executes the silent installation, and immediately deletes the temporary setup files. No personal or vocal data is transmitted during this download.

7. Accessibility by Design

Accessible Tech Labs India engineers software with accessibility as a core requirement, providing full compatibility with NVDA, JAWS, and Windows Narrator through standard keyboard navigation, accessible sliders, read-only shortcut guides, and private speaker voice announcements.

All speech announcements (such as voice profile names spoken when pressing Alt + Shift + Right or Left Arrow) are generated locally using the built-in Windows Speech API (SAPI) on your physical speakers and are never transmitted over the internet or into your live call.

8. Security Principles and Microsoft Store Distribution

Security is a primary engineering requirement across all Accessible Tech Labs India products. VoiceMorph Studio is distributed as a digitally signed MSIX package through the official Microsoft Store, ensuring binary integrity, clean installation, and protection against unauthorized tampering.

We continuously audit our codebase to maintain a zero-telemetry, local-first security posture that safeguards user sovereignty.

9. Third-Party Components and External Links

VoiceMorph Studio utilizes the open-source NAudio library and standard Windows CoreAudio / Media Foundation APIs for local audio processing, alongside optional integration with the VB-Audio Virtual Cable driver for live call routing.

When you voluntarily click external links in the Settings window (such as our official donation support page or privacy policy page), those links open in your default web browser. Accessible Tech Labs India does not share personal user data with any third-party service.

10. International Users

VoiceMorph Studio is available to users worldwide through the Microsoft Store. Because the application collects zero personal or biometric data and performs all vocal processing locally on your device, it inherently complies with global data protection and privacy standards across all jurisdictions.
