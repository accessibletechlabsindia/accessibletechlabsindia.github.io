VoiceMorph Studio

VoiceMorph Studio is the flagship real-time vocal transformation, dual-side live call recording, and studio voice privacy platform engineered by Accessible Tech Labs India. Built from the ground up on modern .NET 10 and C# digital signal processing (DSP) architecture, this enterprise-grade desktop utility delivers zero-latency voice morphing, instant WhatsApp voice note creation, and 100% screen-reader accessibility in one unified offline ecosystem.

100% Screen-Reader Safe Audio Architecture: Unlike conventional voice changers that hijack system speakers and silence screen readers, VoiceMorph Studio features an exclusive Automatic Virtual Cable Parking Engine. The virtual audio bridge remains safely disabled in standby whenever Live Mode is off, guaranteeing that NVDA, JAWS, and Windows Narrator audio never leave your physical speakers or headphones—even across system restarts.

Whether you need natural vocal privacy during live calls on WhatsApp Desktop, Telegram, Zoom, or Google Meet, want to record both sides of an important call without system lag, or wish to send studio-morphed voice notes with a single keyboard shortcut, VoiceMorph Studio provides complete vocal control with zero cloud dependency.

Real-Time Live Voice Changer for Calls (Alt + L)

VoiceMorph Studio routes your transformed voice directly into WhatsApp Desktop Calls, Telegram Calls, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, and Discord in real time. To prevent distracting echo while you speak on a call, your morphed voice is sent exclusively to the calling application while your physical speakers remain dedicated to hearing the other person and your screen reader.

Equipped with 12 Natural and Studio Offline Voice Profiles, our custom granular pitch-shifting and BiQuad formant-shaping DSP engine transforms your vocal identity smoothly without metallic or robotic artifacts.

  • 12 Built-In Vocal Profiles: Includes Natural Deep Male (Smooth Baritone), Natural Soft Female (Warm & Clear), Commanding Deep Male, Bright Young Female, Natural Young Adult, Mature Wise Elder, Podcast & Radio Studio Host, Anonymous Witness (Identity Shield), Telephone / Walkie-Talkie Filter, Stadium Hall Announcer, Soft Stealth Whisper, and Custom User Voice.
  • Global Background Hotkeys: Control your voice from anywhere in Windows while your app is hidden in the system tray—toggle Live Mode (Alt + Shift + L), switch to Next or Previous Voice (Alt + Shift + Right / Left Arrow), or instantly Mute/Unmute your call microphone (Alt + Shift + X).
  • Private Earphone Voice Announcer: When switching voices during a live call using global hotkeys, VoiceMorph Studio announces the newly selected profile name and first/last list boundaries in English exclusively in your physical speakers without leaking a single sound into your call.
  • One-Click Silent Driver Manager: Automatically installs, verifies, or cleanly uninstalls the Virtual Microphone Driver (VB-Cable) in safe silent mode with complete speaker protection.

Dual-Side Live Call Recorder (Alt + C / Alt + Shift + R)

VoiceMorph Studio includes a built-in Dual-Side Live Call Recorder that captures both your outgoing morphed voice and the incoming voice of the person on the other end of the call simultaneously in crystal-clear stereo.

Engineered with a lock-free Direct-to-Disk Background Streaming pipeline and WASAPI Loopback synchronization, call audio is written continuously to disk rather than accumulating in system RAM. Whether your call lasts 5 minutes or 5 hours, VoiceMorph Studio uses minimal memory and never freezes or slows down your computer.

  • Smart Live-Mode Guard: Call recording is strictly interlocked with Live Mode so accidental empty recordings never occur.
  • Automatic Call Finalization: Stopping Live Mode or exiting the application while a call recording is active automatically finalizes and saves your recording in MP3 (192 kbps Stereo) or lossless WAV format.
  • Instant Folder Access (Alt + O): Open your Saved Audio Notes and Call Recordings folder directly from the main window with a single keystroke.

WhatsApp Voice Note Studio and Studio Mastering Controls

Need to send a morphed voice note on WhatsApp or Telegram without starting a live call? Open the dedicated Voice Note Studio (Alt + R) to record, preview, fine-tune, and copy your transformed audio file directly to the Windows Clipboard. Simply switch to WhatsApp Desktop or Telegram and press Ctrl + V to send your voice note immediately.

You can also test your voice anytime using the Quick 10-Second Voice Check (Alt + T) or convert pre-recorded audio files from your computer using Transform Existing Audio File (Alt + F).

  • Precision Vocal Sliders: Fine-tune Voice Pitch (-12 to +12 Semitones), Vocal Timbre (-80% to +80%), and Mic Output Boost (20% to 300%).
  • Dynamic Studio Echo Engine: Enable Studio Voice Echo in Settings and adjust the dynamic Echo Intensity Level slider (10% to 100%) while listening to your voice live in Self-Test Preview.
  • Noise Gate, Peak Limiter & 3 Clarity Modes: Eliminate room and fan noise with the automatic Noise Gate, prevent clipping at high boost levels with the Studio Peak Limiter, and choose between Natural Balanced, Crisp HD Speech, and Warm Studio Bass mastering modes.
