Smart YouTube Search Engine with Real Google Voice Search

ATL Media Downloader features an integrated Smart YouTube Search Engine that lets you discover up to 200 relevant videos instantly inside the application. You no longer need to deal with cluttered web browsers, advertisements, or manual link copying.

Equipped with Real Google Voice Search (Shortcut: Alt + V), you can search YouTube completely hands-free in both English and Hindi. Instant audio cues notify you when the microphone is listening and when your speech is recognized, while intelligent voice-activity detection automatically executes your search the moment you finish speaking.

  • Hands-Free Voice Search (Alt + V): Powered by Google Speech Recognition with real-time audio feedback and automatic silence detection.
  • Rich Accessible Metadata: Every search result announces the video title, exact Channel Name, and natural human-readable Duration (for example, "1 hour, 21 minutes and 5 seconds").
  • Instant Action Context Menu: Press Enter on any result to immediately Play Media, Download Media, or Copy the Video URL.

Sovereign Media Player (Chromium HTML5 and Web Audio Engine)

In Version 1.4, the integrated Sovereign Media Player has been completely re-engineered on a modern Chromium HTML5 and Web Audio API architecture, replacing legacy VLC components. It streams YouTube videos, playlists, and channels directly in a 100% ad-free, distraction-free native window with studio-grade audio clarity and 1080p Full HD visual output.

Designed for zero-latency responsiveness, the player buffers streams intelligently in memory so you can rewind or fast-forward across multi-hour lectures or music tracks instantaneously without playback resets or stuttering.

  • Zero-Latency Seeking: Instant 10-second Rewind and Forward controls via Left and Right Arrow keys.
  • Accessible Real-Time Video Time Slider: Interactive timeline slider that updates continuously and announces natural elapsed and total time (hours, minutes, and seconds) to screen readers.
  • 400% Studio Audio Limiter: Built-in Web Audio DynamicsCompressorNode brickwall limiter boosts quiet recordings up to 400% volume with zero audio clipping or distortion.
  • Variable Playback Speeds: Cycle smoothly between 1.0x, 1.5x, 2.0x, 3.0x, and 4.0x speeds with pitch-preserved speech clarity.
  • Full Playlist Progression: Navigate Previous (P) and Next (N) tracks seamlessly, with optional automatic playback progression.
  • Single-Instance Smart Switching: Selecting a new video from search automatically closes any previous player session so audio streams never overlap.

Enterprise Download Engine and Accessible Live Queue

ATL Media Downloader pairs state-of-the-art extraction engines with multi-threaded FFmpeg processors to deliver lossless audio and high-definition video downloads from over 1,000 platforms. Our streamlined, single-screen Format and Quality dialog lets you choose both your container format and target quality in one unified step.

Built specifically for independent screen reader operation, the Download Progress Window features a keyboard-focusable Live Download Status and Queue List. Without needing NVDA Object Navigation, users can simply press Up and Down Arrow keys to inspect overall progress, real-time speed, estimated time remaining (ETA), active FFmpeg conversion stages, completed counters, and individual video statuses.

  • High-Definition Video Formats: Download in MP4, MKV, and WEBM across 4K Ultra HD (2160p), 1080p Full HD, 720p HD, 480p, and 360p Data Saver resolutions.
  • Studio-Grade Audio Extraction: Convert media into MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OPUS, and AAC at bitrates up to 320 kbps.
  • Selective Playlist and Channel Scanner: Scan entire channels or playlists with full channel name and natural duration announcements, then download selected items or the entire collection into an organized subfolder.
  • Fault-Tolerant Queue & Smart Bypass: Supports up to 10 concurrent threads with multi-client anti-throttling bypass. Unavailable or private playlist videos are safely skipped without interrupting the rest of your download queue.
  • Pause, Resume, and Safe Cancel: Pause and resume active downloads at any time, with safe cancellation prompts defaulting to "No" to prevent accidental aborts and automatic cleanup of partial files.
  • Metadata, Thumbnails, and Subtitles: Optionally embed original cover art, metadata tags, and English/Hindi subtitles directly into downloaded files.
