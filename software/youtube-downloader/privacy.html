ATL Media Downloader Privacy Policy

Enterprise Privacy Framework • Zero-Knowledge Architecture • Data Sovereignty by Design

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

Privacy First Commitment: ATL Media Downloader has been engineered around a strict Zero-Knowledge Architecture. Your files, downloads, voice and text search queries, playback activity, media preferences, and locally processed content remain under your exclusive control and are never transmitted to Accessible Tech Labs India for analysis, storage, profiling, or monetization.

Accessible Tech Labs India is committed to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and digital sovereignty of every user. This Privacy Policy explains how ATL Media Downloader operates, how local and network operations are handled, how your information remains protected, and the architectural principles that govern the application. Unlike modern software platforms that rely on cloud surveillance, telemetry collection, behavioral profiling, and centralized data harvesting, ATL Media Downloader is built on a foundational principle: your data belongs exclusively to you.

ATL Media Downloader is not a surveillance tool, advertising network, analytics tracker, or behavioral profiling system. The application is engineered strictly to provide professional multimedia downloading, hands-free voice discovery, ad-free streaming, and accessible media management while eliminating unnecessary data exposure.

1. Zero-Knowledge Architecture

ATL Media Downloader operates under a strict Zero-Knowledge Architecture model. Under this architecture, Accessible Tech Labs India does not possess the technical capability or server infrastructure to inspect, monitor, recover, analyze, or access the media content searched, streamed, or downloaded through the application on your device.

All media acquisition and processing operations occur directly within your local Windows environment. URL validation, playlist scanning, format conversion, metadata embedding, audio limiting, and file saving are executed locally on your computer without routing your private activity through servers owned by Accessible Tech Labs India.

Because our architecture eliminates centralized user databases, Accessible Tech Labs India maintains zero repositories containing your download history, watch history, voice recordings, personal media libraries, channel selections, or local folder structures.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

ATL Media Downloader does not collect, store, log, sell, rent, license, monetize, or share any of the following categories of personal or behavioral information:

  • Names, usernames, or personal identities
  • Email addresses or phone numbers
  • Passwords, browser cookies, or account credentials
  • Banking, UPI, or payment card details
  • Government identification numbers
  • Downloaded audio or video libraries
  • Media playback history, seek positions, or watch durations
  • Text or voice search histories
  • Permanent microphone or voice recordings
  • Personal files, folders, or private documents
  • Geographic location or IP tracking logs
  • Behavioral analytics or usage telemetry profiles
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking tokens
  • Biometric data, contacts, or private communications

Accessible Tech Labs India does not embed third-party advertisements inside ATL Media Downloader and never sells user data to third parties.

3. Local Device Processing and Configuration Storage

ATL Media Downloader is engineered around local-first execution. Media extraction and high-speed format conversions are performed directly on your machine using local processing engines (yt-dlp and FFmpeg) stored within your user profile directory.

Application preferences—such as your chosen visual theme, default download folder path, concurrent thread count, subtitle preferences, and autoplay settings—are stored solely on your computer inside a local JSON configuration file located in your Windows Local Application Data directory.

Downloaded media files are saved exclusively to the local storage folders you designate. Accessible Tech Labs India never uploads your files, synchronizes your media to cloud servers, or creates hidden remote backups.

4. Smart Search and Voice Search (Microphone) Privacy

ATL Media Downloader includes an integrated Smart YouTube Search Engine supporting both keyboard text input and hands-free Real Google Voice Search (Shortcut: Alt + V).

Microphone Access and Transient Voice Processing: The application never accesses your microphone in the background. Microphone capture is initiated strictly when you explicitly activate the Voice Search button or press the Alt + V shortcut. During a voice search session, a short audio clip is temporarily recorded in your local Windows temporary folder, transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection to Google's Speech Recognition API solely to convert your spoken words into search text, and immediately and permanently deleted from your computer the instant recognition completes or is cancelled.

Accessible Tech Labs India never receives, intercepts, stores, or listens to your voice audio. Neither text queries nor voice transcripts are logged, stored, or used to build user profiles.

5. Sovereign Media Player and Ad-Blocking Privacy

The integrated Sovereign Media Player utilizes a modern Chromium HTML5 and Web Audio API runtime (powered by Microsoft Edge WebView2) to stream media directly in an isolated, distraction-free window.

To protect your privacy and ensure an uninterrupted experience, the Sovereign Media Player incorporates built-in network filters that actively block requests to known advertising and tracking domains (including DoubleClick, Google Syndication, and page-ad telemetry endpoints). Playback speeds, volume amplification settings (via the local Web Audio limiter), timeline positions, and playlist queues remain strictly in local memory on your device and are never reported to Accessible Tech Labs India.

When you close the player window, active media sessions are immediately terminated.

6. Media Download Privacy

When you initiate a download from YouTube or any of the 1,000+ supported platforms, your computer communicates directly with the respective media hosting platform to retrieve the requested audio or video streams.

Accessible Tech Labs India does not act as a proxy server, does not intercept your network traffic, and does not maintain any record of the URLs you paste, the channels you scan, or the formats you select.

If a download is cancelled, ATL Media Downloader automatically cleans up temporary partial files (.part and .ytdl) from your target folder to keep your storage clean.

7. Core Engine Security Updates

To prevent extraction errors and maintain compatibility with evolving media platform protocols, ATL Media Downloader includes an automated Update Manager.

On startup, the Update Manager queries the official public GitHub release API for the core extraction engine (yt-dlp) to verify whether a newer version is available. This check transmits only a standard HTTP version request and does not transmit any personal data, hardware identifiers, or usage history.

When an update is authorized, the core engine binary is updated directly from its official source to ensure continuous, secure functionality.

8. Accessibility by Design

Accessible Tech Labs India engineers software with accessibility as a core requirement, providing full compatibility with NVDA, JAWS, and Windows Narrator through standard keyboard navigation, live status lists, accessible sliders, and clear auditory feedback.

All accessibility enhancements operate locally within the Windows Forms and UI Automation framework on your computer. We never track or record whether a user is operating assistive technology.

9. Security Principles

Security is a primary engineering requirement across all Accessible Tech Labs India products. ATL Media Downloader is distributed as a digitally signed package through the official Microsoft Store, ensuring binary integrity and protection against tampering.

While no software connected to the internet can guarantee immunity against every theoretical threat, we continuously audit our codebase to minimize attack surfaces and safeguard user sovereignty.

10. Third-Party Components and External Links

ATL Media Downloader integrates trusted, industry-standard components—including yt-dlp, FFmpeg, YoutubeDLSharp, and the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime—to execute local extraction, media conversion, and HTML5 playback.

When you interact with external media platforms (such as YouTube) or voluntarily open external links in your web browser (such as our official UPI support page), those external services are governed by their own respective privacy policies. Accessible Tech Labs India does not share personal user data with any third-party component.

11. International Users

ATL Media Downloader is available to users worldwide through the Microsoft Store. Because the application collects zero personal data and performs all processing locally on your device, it inherently respects global data protection and data minimization standards across all jurisdictions.
