Media Composer Engine

Media Composer Engine is the next-generation, premium media processing suite developed by Accessible Tech Labs India. Engineered from the ground up with a strict accessibility-first architecture, it delivers a seamless and robust experience for visually impaired users relying on screen readers like NVDA and JAWS. This enterprise-grade software empowers users with over 20 advanced, smart media tools within a highly intuitive and completely keyboard-navigable environment.

Whether you require cinematic video stabilization, AI-powered vocal extraction, dynamic 2D to 3D dimension architecture, or smart automatic silence removal, this engine handles complex production tasks with unprecedented speed and precision. Built for professional content creators, audio engineers, and everyday users who demand uncompromised quality, Media Composer Engine redefines the boundaries of accessible software.

Core Enterprise Features & Toolset

Media Composer Engine combines heavy-duty multimedia frameworks with a zero-bloat, distraction-free interface. The suite includes:

  • AI Studio Vocal & Track Extractor: Utilize advanced local AI models to isolate vocals, drums, bass, and melody into separate stems from any audio or video file.
  • Pro Studio Video Recorder: Capture up to 4K 60fps cinematic video with live Chroma Key (Green Screen) removal, real-time color grading, and automatic lighting fixes.
  • Pro Studio Audio Recorder: Record uncompressed 48kHz WAV audio with real-time AI background noise removal, auto-normalization, and intelligent silence cutting.
  • Slowed & Reverb Lofi Maker: Transform regular tracks into cinematic Lofi mixes with adjustable speed multipliers and deep echo algorithms.
  • Smart Spatial Controller: Generate true 8D spatial audio with 360-degree rotation capabilities, or selectively mute left/right audio channels.
  • Absolute Privacy & Security: 100% offline local processing with zero telemetry. Your camera feeds, microphone inputs, and media files never leave your machine.
  • Next-Generation Accessibility: Deep UI Automation integration ensures every slider, combobox, and progress bar announces correctly to your screen reader.

We have integrated an offline Demucs AI Engine. You can now extract pristine acapellas, karaoke tracks, isolated drums, or individual bass lines directly from any media file without relying on expensive cloud subscriptions. It works entirely offline using your machine's hardware.

Studio-Grade Capture Modules

The new Pro Studio Recorders allow you to capture 4K Video and 24-bit Audio directly within the engine. Features include live green-screen removal, cinematic color grading, AI noise reduction, and auto-normalization, bypassing the need for external recording software.

Advanced Spatial & Lofi Engines

Create atmospheric content instantly. The Slowed & Reverb Maker batches multiple files into cinematic Lofi mixes, while the Spatial Controller allows you to render immersive 8D audio with customizable rotation speeds for an unparalleled listening experience.
