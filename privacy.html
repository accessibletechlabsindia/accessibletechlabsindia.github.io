Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Accessible Tech Labs India handles information when you visit this website, use our browser-based tools, download or use our software, use our games, access our training resources, or contact us through an official communication channel.

We design our services with accessibility, data minimisation, user control, and local processing in mind. However, some features connect to external services. This policy identifies those situations so that you can make an informed decision before using a feature.

1. Scope of this Policy

This Policy applies to the Accessible Tech Labs India website hosted at accessibletechlabsindia.github.io and to the web tools, software information pages, games, training resources, and add-on resources linked from it.

Third-party websites, APIs, download hosts, communication platforms, libraries, and services linked from this website have their own privacy policies and terms. Their practices are not controlled by Accessible Tech Labs India.

2. Information We May Receive

Depending on how you use the website, information may include the content that you voluntarily send to us by email, WhatsApp, Telegram, or another communication channel; technical information processed by GitHub Pages and content delivery networks; and information sent to an external service when you intentionally use a tool that requires that service.

We do not require an account to browse the main website. We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a support, collaboration, training, or other communication request.

3. Local Browser Processing

Several tools are designed to process data in your browser or on your device. Examples may include text conversion, PDF processing, audio processing, image processing, calculations, and some games.

For these tools, files and text may remain in the browser memory while the feature is running. You should close the page and clear the tool interface after use, especially when working with confidential information.

Local processing is not a guarantee that your browser, operating system, extensions, network provider, or device is free from monitoring. Do not use any website tool for information that you are not authorised to process.

4. Features That Use External Services

Some tools send limited information to external providers in order to perform the requested function. The exact data sent depends on the feature and the information entered by you.

  • DNS and network tools may send a domain name or network query to DNS and related providers.
  • Weather tools may send a location search, coordinates, or browser-approved location to geocoding and weather providers.
  • Translation tools may send entered text or extracted document text to the translation provider.
  • Image or OCR tools may use browser libraries locally, but you should verify the current page behaviour before submitting sensitive files.
  • Password checking may send a partial cryptographic hash prefix to a breach-checking provider. Do not enter a password that you use for an important account.
  • Email breach checking may send the email address entered by you to the selected breach-checking service.
  • Temporary inbox tools may create an account with the temporary email provider and may send or retrieve inbox information through that provider.
  • File-transfer tools may upload a file to the external transfer provider selected by the tool.
  • Clipboard or payload-transfer tools may transmit encrypted or encoded content to the messaging service used by that feature.
  • GitHub intelligence tools may query public GitHub profile information through the GitHub API.

External providers may process data under their own policies, retain logs, impose rate limits, or change their services. Review the provider's policy before using a feature that sends data outside your device.

5. External Libraries and Content Delivery

Some pages may load libraries, fonts, or other resources from services such as unpkg.com, raw.githubusercontent.com, Google-hosted services, or other third-party content delivery networks. When a browser requests an external resource, the provider may receive technical request information such as an IP address, browser information, requested resource, and time of request.

We do not control the privacy practices, availability, security, or retention policies of those providers. Where practical, we may replace external dependencies with locally hosted files in future updates.

6. Cookies, Storage, and Analytics

The main website is not intentionally designed around advertising cookies, behavioural advertising, or user accounts. Some pages or third-party services may nevertheless use their own cookies, local storage, session storage, cache, or similar browser technologies.

Browser storage can be used by a tool to retain temporary interface state or preferences. Clearing the page, closing the browser, or using the browser's site-data controls may remove locally stored information, depending on the feature.

GitHub Pages, hosting infrastructure, security systems, browsers, internet service providers, and external APIs may maintain technical logs as part of operating and protecting their services. We do not control those logs.

7. Contact and Support Communications

If you contact us by email, WhatsApp, Telegram, YouTube, or another channel, the platform and our communication account may receive your name, username, phone number, email address, message, attachments, and technical details included in your communication.

We use this information to respond to your request, provide support, investigate reports, improve accessibility, address abuse, and maintain records needed for legitimate operational or legal purposes.

Do not send passwords, recovery codes, government identity documents, payment card details, private keys, or confidential files through an ordinary support message.

8. Downloads and Desktop Software

Software downloaded from this website may connect to services required by the software, such as update servers, public repositories, operating-system services, media providers, or other providers described in the software documentation.

A desktop program may also create files, logs, settings, caches, or temporary data on your device. The exact behaviour depends on the program, version, operating system, permissions, and configuration. Read the product-specific documentation before installation.

The website privacy policy cannot guarantee the behaviour of an independently downloaded executable, third-party dependency, operating-system component, or release host.

9. Training and Registration Information

If you contact us about training, a course, a batch, certification, or participation, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, contact details, accessibility requirements, educational information, and communication preferences.

We use that information to respond to enquiries, manage participation, communicate schedules, provide learning support, and issue or verify course-related information where applicable.

We do not require you to disclose disability or medical information. Provide only the minimum information necessary for the requested support.

10. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate and maintain the website, respond to enquiries, provide technical support, improve accessibility and reliability, investigate security incidents, prevent misuse, distribute updates, manage training communications, comply with applicable law, and protect the rights and safety of users and the project.

We do not intentionally sell personal information or use support messages for behavioural advertising. We do not make decisions about a person's eligibility, employment, credit, insurance, or access to essential services using automated profiling on this website.

11. Sharing and Disclosure

We may disclose information when you direct us to do so, when a third-party feature is required to provide the function you requested, to service providers supporting website operations, to protect users and infrastructure, to investigate abuse or security issues, or when disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

We do not promise that information sent to a third-party provider will remain exclusively in India. The provider may process information in other countries according to its own terms and policies.

12. Retention

We retain communication information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, provide support, maintain operational records, resolve disputes, protect the project, or comply with legal obligations.

Temporary data created by browser tools may remain in browser memory, browser storage, download folders, external providers, or device backups until it is deleted by the relevant user, browser, device, or provider. Retention periods of external services are governed by their policies.

13. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to a static public website and seek to minimise unnecessary data collection. However, no website, software, communication platform, API, download, or internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

You are responsible for using supported software, maintaining device security, reviewing permissions, verifying downloads, using strong unique passwords, and avoiding confidential information in tools that use external services.

14. Children and Young Users

The website is not intended to collect personal information from children without appropriate involvement of a parent, guardian, school, or responsible organisation. If you believe that a child has sent personal information to us unnecessarily, contact us so that we can review the request.

15. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law and your circumstances, you may ask what personal information we hold about your communication, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion where legally available, withdraw a voluntary request, or ask questions about a feature's data flow.

To make a privacy request, contact us using the details on our Contact & Community page. We may need enough information to identify the relevant communication and prevent unauthorised requests.

16. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the website, tools, software, external services, legal requirements, or security practices change. The effective date and last-updated date at the top of this page will be changed when a material update is published.

You should review this page before using a feature that handles personal, confidential, or sensitive information.

17. Acceptance and Responsible Use

By continuing to use this website after reading this Policy, you acknowledge the practices described here. Where a feature requires a separate consent, permission, or confirmation, that requirement applies in addition to this Policy.

If you do not agree with the applicable privacy practices, do not use the relevant website feature and do not submit personal or confidential information through it.
