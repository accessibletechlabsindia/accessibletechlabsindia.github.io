The Continuous Optical Intelligence Engine

The Universal Visual Assistant is a high-performance, real-time optical processing workstation engineered by Accessible Tech Labs India. Designed to seamlessly integrate into the daily lives of the Visually Impaired (VI) community, this utility transforms your device's camera into a continuous, intelligent sensory radar. It features three distinct operational modes: a highly accurate Color Detector utilizing center-point RGB averaging, a Live Document Reader employing advanced optical character recognition to continuously scan and vocalize text without requiring repetitive inputs, and an Ambient Light Radar that translates environmental luminance into intuitive acoustic frequencies. Adhering strictly to our Zero-Knowledge architecture, all optical data streams are processed entirely within the volatile memory of your local browser. No video frames, images, or text are ever transmitted, cached, or stored on external servers, guaranteeing absolute digital sovereignty and privacy. Built upon a 'Screen Reader First' framework, the interface utilizes precise ARIA-live management, enterprise-grade Web Audio API telemetry, and fluid keyboard navigation. Empowering you to perceive your physical environment with unparalleled accuracy, speed, and uncompromising independence.
