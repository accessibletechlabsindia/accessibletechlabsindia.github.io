Course Methodology & Infrastructure

Our pedagogical architecture is designed for maximum practical impact. We reject the traditional model of rote memorization. Instead, our training relies heavily on analyzing real-world cyber threat scenarios, live practical demonstrations, active group discussions, and continuous awareness activities. The structured framework of our 2-month program includes:

  • Language of Instruction: All live sessions are conducted strictly in Hindi to ensure profound conceptual clarity, deep engagement, and absolute comprehension for our core demographic.
  • Platform & Schedule: Live interactive classes are hosted seamlessly via Google Meet from Monday to Saturday, specifically scheduled between 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM IST (Sunday Holiday).
  • Weekly Assessments: Every Saturday is dedicated to rigorous Weekly Assessments. This allows us to continuously evaluate learner progress and solidify the critical concepts discussed throughout the week.
  • Secure Recording Access: We understand that true technical mastery requires repetitive review. Therefore, high-quality audio and video recordings of every single class are securely uploaded and organized via Google Drive. Learners hold exclusive access to these archives to reinforce their understanding at any time.
  • Official Certification: Upon the successful completion of the rigorous two-month cycle and passing the required assessments, eligible participants are awarded a highly respected Certificate of Completion officially issued by Accessible Tech Labs India.

Comprehensive Syllabus: Basic to Advanced Architecture

Phase 1: Basic to Advanced Computer Mastery

This module engineers a robust technical foundation, scaling from absolute basics to advanced power-user operations. Learners master Computer Fundamentals, Advanced Windows OS Navigation, Precision Keyboard Workflows, and Universal Shortcuts. The curriculum elevates everyday tasks by teaching Advanced File and Folder Management, Secure Copy/Paste protocols, complex ZIP/UnZIP archiving mechanics, Deep Internet Browsing architectures, and Enterprise-level Email Management.

Phase 2: Basic to Advanced Cyber Safety & Digital Defense

Moving far beyond basic internet safety, this phase equips learners with an elite, enterprise-level security mindset. We dissect the psychology of modern threats, teaching students to neutralize Advanced Social Engineering and Phishing attacks. The curriculum dives deep into Digital Footprint Management, Cryptographic Password Security, Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) deployment, HTTP vs. HTTPS protocol analysis, Email and Social Media Security, Digital Reputation Privacy, Safe Software Download Protocols, Fake/Pirated Software Detection, Honeypot mechanics, and fundamental Digital Forensics.
