Course Methodology & Infrastructure

Our pedagogical architecture is designed for maximum practical impact. We reject the traditional model of rote memorization. Instead, our training relies heavily on analyzing real-world cyber threat scenarios, live practical demonstrations, active group discussions, and continuous awareness activities. The structured framework of our 3-month program includes:

  • Language of Instruction: All live sessions are conducted strictly in Hindi to ensure profound conceptual clarity, deep engagement, and absolute comprehension for our core demographic.
  • Platform & Schedule: Live interactive classes are hosted seamlessly via Google Meet from Monday to Saturday, specifically scheduled between 5:00 PM to 6:00 PM IST (Sunday Holiday).
  • Weekly Assessments: Every Saturday is dedicated to rigorous Weekly Assessments. This allows us to continuously evaluate learner progress and solidify the critical concepts discussed throughout the week.
  • Secure Recording Access: We understand that true technical mastery requires repetitive review. Therefore, high-quality audio and video recordings of every single class are securely uploaded and organized via Google Drive. Learners hold exclusive access to these archives to reinforce their understanding at any time.
  • Official Certification: Upon the successful completion of the rigorous three-month cycle and passing the required assessments, eligible participants are awarded a highly respected Certificate of Completion officially issued by Accessible Tech Labs India.

Comprehensive Syllabus: Basic to Advanced Architecture

Phase 1: Basic to Advanced Computer Mastery & Office Productivity

This module engineers an uncompromising technical foundation, scaling from absolute beginner fundamentals to advanced enterprise productivity. Learners master deep Windows operating system navigation, system settings architecture, precision keyboard workflows, and screen-reader shortcut efficiency.

The curriculum includes complete, hands-on training across the Microsoft Office Suite: professional document structuring, styling, and formatting in Microsoft Word; workbook architecture, cell operations, and complex computational logic in Microsoft Excel utilizing advanced formulas such as VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, IF/IFS, SUMIFS, and statistical operators; and structured presentation design in Microsoft PowerPoint.

Additionally, students master Command Prompt (CLI) Administration, batch scripting basics, automated system maintenance, archive compression mechanics, deep internet browsing research, and the strategic integration of modern Assistive Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools to dramatically accelerate daily academic and professional tasks.

Phase 2: Basic to Advanced Cyber Safety & Digital Defense

Moving far beyond conventional internet tips, this phase establishes an elite, enterprise-level cybersecurity posture. We thoroughly deconstruct the psychological mechanics of modern digital crime, training students to detect and neutralize advanced Social Engineering, Phishing, Vishing, and Impersonation Frauds.

Specialized modules are dedicated to Digital Payment and UPI Fraud Defense, educating learners on identifying malicious QR codes, deceptive payment requests, banking Trojan vectors, and SIM-swap vulnerabilities. Students learn defensive account architecture through Cryptographic Password Hygiene and hardware-level Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA / 2FA) implementation.

The training dives deep into Network Security, distinguishing secure HTTPS channels, isolating public Wi-Fi risks, deploying VPNs, detecting fake or pirated software installations, understanding honeypot mechanics, safeguarding personal privacy, and grasping foundational digital forensic trace methodologies.
