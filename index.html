The Three Architectural Pillars of ATLI

1. Absolute Zero-Knowledge Privacy

We categorically refuse to turn users into commodities. Our tools run on local browser memory and offline runtimes. Your personal documents, photos, and configurations never touch remote databases.

2. Screen Reader First Engineering

Accessibility is our foundation, not an afterthought. Every interface is tested with NVDA, JAWS, and TalkBack, featuring logical keyboard hierarchies, ARIA live regions, and high-contrast support.

3. Purely Independent & Non-Profit

We operate without corporate funding, venture capital, advertising trackers, or paywalls. Our work is driven by community empowerment, technological equality, and genuine human impact.

About the Laboratory & Our Movement

Accessible Tech Labs India began on November 9, 2025, as a dedicated YouTube initiative broadcasting digital safety tutorials to visually impaired users. In 2026, under the vision of Founder and System Architect Krishna Kumar alongside a committed core team of five engineers (Krishna Kumar, Anil Saini, Sumit Kumar, Gauri, and Haseena), we expanded into an active software engineering laboratory.

Today, our ecosystem delivers high-performance Windows applications, certified screen reader add-ons, accessible assessment games, and comprehensive two-month Cyber Safety Training batches completely free of charge. We believe that true digital freedom begins when technology adapts to the human being, rather than forcing the human being to adapt to technology.
